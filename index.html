What the 9-box grid is

The 9-box grid is a talent management tool that plots every employee on two axes — Performance (what they deliver today) and Potential (how far they could grow) — to identify who to develop, stretch, reward, or manage out. It gives a shared, objective language for talent reviews and points development budget where it will have the most impact.

Four steps to build it (AIHR framework)

1. Determine your targetFocus on roles critical to the business, hard-to-fill specialist roles, and senior roles tied to succession planning — rather than applying the grid universally.
2. Define performance criteriaUse SMART, measurable indicators where possible; balance quantitative targets with qualitative signals like soft skills; benchmark against industry practice.
3. Define potential criteriaClarify what "potential" means at Bugloos specifically, and support each rating with concrete evidence rather than gut feel.
4. Validate & visualizeCheck the data for inconsistencies and outliers before plotting it, then keep the visualization simple and uncluttered.

Performance criteria

LevelCriteriaIndicators
LowDoesn't align with job requirements; misses personal targets.Consistently falls short of monthly targets; regular feedback about unmet job duties.
MediumAligns to some extent with job demands; achieves some but not all objectives.Meets some deadlines but struggles with others; proficient in some skills, lacking in others.
HighFully meets job requirements and individual targets.Regularly exceeds targets; demonstrates leadership and initiative.

In this grid, performance level is derived from each employee's Final score % (the sheet's blended Team + Individual Performance figure) where it's available (≥95% High, 58–94% Medium, <58% Low), and otherwise falls back to the sheet's "Meets / Doesn't Meet expectations" rating (Medium / Low).

Potential criteria

LevelDefinition
LowWorking at full capacity in the current role; not expected to grow further (ceiling reached, or limited motivation).
MediumRoom to develop further within the current role — in performance, skills, or expertise.
HighPerforms well beyond the expectations of the role and naturally takes on leadership.

Potential level comes from each employee's Potential score / Total Potential rating already recorded in the appraisal sheet (High ≥4.0, Medium 2.5–3.99, Low ≤2.5). Ratings are dynamic — someone rated "low potential" today can move up with the right development.

Best practices

  • Define clear criteria — spell out what performance and potential mean at Bugloos and set concrete benchmarks, to reduce bias and set clear expectations.
  • Consult stakeholders — involve managers, HR, and employees so the grid's purpose and methodology have buy-in, and use it as part of a broader talent management practice.
  • Test and refine — pilot with a smaller group, gather feedback, and adjust before rolling out company-wide.
  • Ensure data privacy — handle 9-box results with discretion and use them as a conversation guide, not a public ranking.
